Project B · Parallel primitives · Milestone 5 of 5
A teammate's primitives library is "slow". Find out why, with evidence, and fix what can be fixed.

The brief

The starter contains a small library as shipped, with a runner for each kernel:

RunnerWhat it doesSize
runSum(x, out, n)Sum of a float array into out[0]n = 218
runHist(x, bins, n)16-bin histogram of x & 15 (u32). The real data is skewed: most values land in one binn = 218
runSpeed(p, out, n)2 · vx for particles stored as 8 floats each (x, y, z, vx, vy, vz, mass, id)n = 218
runScan(x, y, n)Inclusive prefix sumn = 216

Each runner returns the array of its launch stats. Keep the signatures; change anything inside. The inputs' layout is fixed: other code produces them.

How it's checked

  • All four runners still correct.
  • At least two of sum, histogram and scan made ≥ 3× faster (total cycles over all launches) than the shipped versions.
  • A write-up: a diagnosis of each kernel, using the Chapter 5.3 procedure and quoting the numbers, before and after. One of the four can't be fixed inside the kernel at all. Say which, prove it with metrics, and say what you'd change instead.
