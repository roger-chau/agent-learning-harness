What it covers

The whole course, with emphasis on Parts 5–9: measurement and diagnosis, the compiler's view (allocation, scheduling, loop transformations), fused operators and attention, streams, numerics, portability and power, plus design problems that combine them.

Like a final at a top university, it rewards reasoning from first principles: every answer can be derived from a few numbers about the machine and the kernel.

How to prepare

  • Pass the midterm first, and revisit any topic where you lost marks.
  • Redo the Part 5–9 problem sets without hints, and rehearse the worked examples out loud: diagnosis from counters, scheduling by hand, online softmax by hand.
  • Bring your lab results to mind: the final asks the kinds of questions your lab write-ups answer.
