Build: a fast SGEMM

Write gemmFast(A, B, C, n) and runFast(A, B, C, n). Start from your C2 kernel and apply what Chapters 4.3 and 4.4 offer: bigger thread tiles, vector loads for the global-to-shared copies, conflict-free shared reads, double buffering if the limiter calls for it. n is a multiple of 64.

Declare registers honestly in your launch config. A thread holding 64 accumulators doesn't fit in 32 registers. If you declare ilp, your loop must really overlap loads with math.

How it's checked

  • Correct on 64×64 and 256×256.
  • ≥ 60% of FP32 peak on 256×256.
  • An optimisation log: the write-up below. Performance engineers keep one for every kernel. It's how you remember why the code looks the way it does, and it's what you'll talk through in interviews.

Make one change at a time and measure each. Record what limitedBy said before and after every change.
