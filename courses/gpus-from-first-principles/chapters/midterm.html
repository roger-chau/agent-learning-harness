What it covers

Parts 0–4: latency, throughput and Little's law; SIMT execution, warps and divergence; the roofline, sectors, coalescing, caches, shared memory, bank conflicts and occupancy; reduction, scan, atomics, the memory model, compaction and sorting; and the GEMM ladder from naive to tensor cores.

The problems are new: none is copied from a problem set. They're weighted toward analysis (★★) over recall, as a university midterm would be.

How to prepare

  • Redo the problem sets of Parts 0–4 without hints, timing yourself at about 6–7 minutes per problem.
  • Be able to derive, not just quote: Little's law for a GPU, the sector count of any access pattern, the occupancy of any configuration, the bank-conflict degree of any stride, and the instruction ceiling of each GEMM step.
  • Have a calculator ready. Exact arithmetic matters: numeric answers are checked to about 1%.
