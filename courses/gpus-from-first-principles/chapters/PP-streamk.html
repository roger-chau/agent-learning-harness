Paper project · Stream-K (Project C)
Remove your GEMM's tail effect.

Build

Using your C3 kernel's inner loop (64 × 64 tiles, k-steps of 16, or your own shape), write two launchers. Each returns the array of stats for every launch it made:

  • runDataParallel(A, B, C, n): one block per output tile.
  • runStreamK(A, B, C, n): one persistent block per SM, each doing an equal range of the total k-iterations, with a fix-up for split tiles. Any correct fix-up is fine: atomicAdd into C, a workspace plus a second kernel, or the paper's flags.

Two practical notes:

  • C arrives full of garbage, as it would on a real GPU. If your fix-up accumulates into C, clear the split tiles first (and count that launch).
  • In the simulator, a helper function can't contain __syncthreads(). Write the tile loop out in each kernel.

How it's checked

  • Both correct at n = 192 (9 tiles: less than one wave on 16 SMs), and Stream-K at least 1.4× faster than your data-parallel version there, counting all its launches.
  • Stream-K correct at n = 320 (25 tiles), with its main kernel's smBalance ≥ 0.95.
