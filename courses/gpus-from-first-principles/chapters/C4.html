Go through the FP32 roof with tensor cores.

Build: a tensor-core GEMM

Write gemmTC(A, B, C, n) and runTC(A, B, C, n). Use __mma_sync for the math, with tiles staged through shared memory. n is a multiple of 64. A good starting shape: 4 warps per block, a 32×32 block tile, each warp owning one 16×16 sub-tile of C.

Your first version will probably run into a different limiter than C3's. Read limitedBy, then reach for Chapter 4.4.

How it's checked

  • Correct on 64×64 and 256×256 within the FP16 error budget $n \cdot \max|A| \cdot \max|B| \cdot 2 \cdot 2^{-11}$ against an exact reference. The test data is chosen so FP16 rounding actually happens.
  • Uses tensor cores (tensorOps > 0).
  • ≥ 200% of the FP32 peak on 256×256, which no FP32 kernel can reach.

As in C3: an ilp you declare must be one your loop really has.
