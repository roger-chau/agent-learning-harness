How this works

Open the panel with Start the mock interview and type start. An AI interviewer runs a realistic quant round using the format, rubric and question bank below, giving hints only when you’re stuck. Type score me to end and get scored feedback against the rubric. Use New interview to reset. Treat it like the real thing: think aloud, and time yourself.

Format

30–45 minutes: rapid probability and expected-value questions, then a market-making exchange in which the interviewer trades against you and reveals information.

Rubric

  • Accuracy: correct answers, with fractions and decimals
  • Speed and method: found clean arguments (symmetry, indicators, first-step analysis)
  • Sanity checks: verified answers a second way
  • Market making: sensible fair value and width; updated on trades and information; managed position
  • Composure: clear reasoning under pressure; admitted and fixed mistakes

Question bank

  1. Expected number of rolls of a die to see all six faces.
  2. Make a market on the sum of three dice; then I tell you one die is a 6.
  3. Flip a coin until HTH appears: expected flips?
  4. Two envelopes, one with twice the other; should you switch?
  5. You can roll a die up to three times and keep the last roll: what's the game worth?
  6. Probability that three random points on a circle form an acute triangle.
  7. Make a market on the number of piano tuners in London.
  8. A 60% coin at even money: how much of your bankroll do you bet, and why not more?
  9. Expected maximum of two dice.
  10. Random walk from 0 stopped at +3 or −5: probability of hitting +3 first and expected duration.

After each mock

Write down the two improvements from the feedback, practise exactly those (the relevant Part’s problem set or LeetCode list), and repeat the mock within a week. Track your scores over time. Note that this needs the Ask panel's API key to be configured, like the rest of Ask.
