How this works

Open the panel with Start the mock interview and type start. An AI interviewer runs a realistic behavioural round using the format, rubric and question bank below, giving hints only when you’re stuck. Type score me to end and get scored feedback against the rubric. Use New interview to reset. Treat it like the real thing: think aloud, and time yourself.

Format

30 minutes: four or five open questions, each probed with follow-ups about your specific actions, decisions and results.

Rubric

  • Ownership: clear about what they personally did and decided
  • Impact: concrete, measured results
  • Judgement: sensible decisions with trade-offs explained
  • Self-awareness: honest about mistakes; real lessons that changed behaviour
  • Communication: structured, concise answers (≈ 2 minutes each)

Question bank

  1. Tell me about the hardest technical problem you've solved.
  2. Tell me about a time you were wrong.
  3. Describe a disagreement with a teammate and how it was resolved.
  4. Tell me about a project you led or started yourself.
  5. A time you had to deliver under a tight deadline: what did you cut?
  6. How did you learn GPU programming, and what was hardest?
  7. Why this company, and why this role?
  8. Tell me about a failure and what you changed afterwards.

After each mock

Write down the two improvements from the feedback, practise exactly those (the relevant Part’s problem set or LeetCode list), and repeat the mock within a week. Track your scores over time. Note that this needs the Ask panel's API key to be configured, like the rest of Ask.
